About

Josiah Salurio

Web developer based in Calbayog City, Samar, Philippines. I build sites and applications for real clients, one project at a time.

I graduated from NorthWest Samar State University with a degree in Entertainment and Multimedia Computing. Over the past year I have built websites and web applications for real clients, covering everything from landing pages to full stack apps with authentication and databases.

My path into development started with the front end (HTML, CSS, and JavaScript) and grew from there into working with SQL, authentication systems, and databases as client projects demanded more than a static page. Each project pushed the work a little further: a travel agency's marketing site, a church's multi page presence, a quiz app with live scoring, a real time chat application.

I am based in Calbayog City, Samar, Philippines, and I work comfortably in both Tagalog and English. I take on freelance work directly with clients and I'm equally comfortable owning a build end to end or slotting into a larger team.

How I work

Approach
01
Start with the brief
Every project starts by understanding what the client actually needs the site or app to do, not just what it should look like.
02
Build lean
I reach for the simplest stack that gets the job done well: a static site when that's enough, a database and auth when it isn't.
03
Mobile first
Most visitors arrive on a phone, so every layout is checked and refined at small widths before anything else.
04
Communicate clearly
Clients get plain-language updates on progress and decisions, in Tagalog or English, whichever is easier for them.
